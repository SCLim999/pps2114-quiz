How to play
  • Lemmings drop out of the hatch and walk until something stops them. They never take orders as a group — you assign a skill to one lemming at a time.
  • Pick a skill in the bar below (or press 1–8), then click a lemming. A green box means the skill can be given; a red box means it cannot.
  • Climber and Floater are permanent abilities. Bomber blows the lemming up after 5 seconds (it also blows a hole in the ground). Blocker stands still and turns everyone else around. Builder lays 12 bricks as a staircase. Basher tunnels sideways, Miner tunnels diagonally down, Digger digs straight down.
  • Falling further than about six body heights is fatal — unless the lemming is a floater. Grey steel cannot be dug through.
  • Reach the exit with enough lemmings before the clock runs out.
